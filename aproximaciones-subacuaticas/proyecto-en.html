    METÁFORA

Aproximaciones subacuáticas (Underwater Approaches) is a sound and sports experimentation project carried out by Club Sonoro Deportivo between 2025 and 2026 at the swimming pool of the Juan Gómez Millas Sports Field.

The project explores the relationship between swimming, listening and sound creation, asking about the sounds that accompany sporting activity and the ways in which the body perceives and produces sound in motion.

Non-professional swimmers were invited to form a group of performers and to take part in monthly sessions of sports and sound training. On the one hand, these sessions focused on refining technique and endurance, as well as shifting the ways of swimming and using the pool; on the other, they focused on deep listening, attention and orientation through sound. This is how we incorporated other ways of crossing or entering the pool, ways of listening through the skin and the body, and the creation of sound maps, scores and onomatopoeias.

The work with the swimmers aimed to expand their underwater perception and, with it, to understand other dimensions of their sporting practice; to offer listening experiences that explore how sound is perceived and interpreted in an underwater environment; and to develop a sound-performance piece, turning the swimmers into performers, the coach into a conductor, and the pool into an instrument.

Each session was a team effort in which, besides training, we recorded and processed sounds captured with hydrophones, and sought to learn forms of visual and sonic communication as well as composition strategies.

One of its outcomes was the public concert Nada como una metáfora, held on March 28 at the Juan Gómez Millas Sports Field.

The research was developed with the support of the Vice-Rectorate for Student and Community Affairs (VAEC), the Department of Sports and Physical Activity (DDAF), the Master's in Media Arts and the Sound Arts Unit (Núcleo de Artes Sonoras) of the Universidad de Chile, as well as the label Archivo Veintidós. It is funded by the National Fund for Cultural and Arts Development (Fondart), National Funding Line, 2025 call.

Club Sonoro Deportivo

Club Sonoro Deportivo (Sound Sports Club) is a collective that brings together people interested in sport and its many forms of approach, with a special emphasis on its sonic dimension. Its members are the artists Fernanda Fábrega Rubio, Matías Serrano Acevedo and Santiago Corvalán Garretón.
